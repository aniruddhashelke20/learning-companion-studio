## 5. Key Discussions

### Discussion 1
* **Topic:** UI Mockup Complexity & Usability
* **Summary:** The student team presented a high-level mock interface featuring video navigation, transcript panels, and engagement questions. The client team remarked that this interface looked like an "absolute academic platform" which would be too complicated for new tutors or parents. They emphasized that the app must be approachable for laypeople and focus heavily on simple tag navigation to identify how tags map to child characteristics.
* **Outcome:** The student team agreed to simplify the layout, focusing on stepwise structures and clear icon maps.

### Discussion 2
* **Topic:** Prototyping Platform & Approvals
* **Summary:** The team discussed the choice of tool for presenting the simplified prototype. Both Figma and Canva were proposed; the student team expressed comfort using Canva. The team also asked how the approval process would work.
* **Outcome:** The client team agreed that Canva is suitable. The student team will design the prototype and send it to the client. The client team will discuss it internally and send approvals/adjustments asynchronously.

### Discussion 3
* **Topic:** Materials and Keywords Supply
* **Summary:** The students requested guidance on the precise tags, icons, and content material. The client lead explained that they are ready to supply specific keywords, templates, and materials back-to-back once the interface prototype is established.
* **Outcome:** The student team will directly request tag definitions or keywords via the WhatsApp group or by contacting Jamsun.
